Delete your account and data

In the game: Settings → Delete account and data. This permanently deletes your account, cloud save, and the gameplay and ad data linked to it from our servers.

If you can't open the game, email orcub@sathuashrith.com from the address you signed in with, or include your player ID from Settings. We complete deletion within 30 days and confirm by email.

Ad partners keep their own records under their own policies; see the privacy policy for links.

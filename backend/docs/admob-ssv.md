# Rewarded life verification

In AdMob, open rewarded unit `ca-app-pub-7345153763598739/3829033868`, then server-side verification settings. Set callback URL to `https://orcub.sathuashrith.com/v1/ads/ssv`, verify, and save. The coordinator owns this console step. Do not supply a fixed user ID or custom data in production: the client sets both per impression.

The client requests `POST /v1/ads/nonce` with player_id and placement (`rewarded_lives`, `out_of_lives`, or `retry_lives`) before showing the ad. The server reserves a random, player/placement-bound nonce, capped at 20 reservations per UTC day. Cancelled impressions consume a reservation: this conservative choice prevents concurrent devices from exceeding the provisional cap. Nonces expire after 24 hours. On reward, the client immediately applies a durable provisional refill; no gold is spent. Spends after that refill carry rewarded_nonce and wait server-side for confirmation (retriable 503). When no spends are queued, wallet polling reconciles rewarded_nonces and applied_keys. Editor simulated ads apply capped provisional refills only; they never mint a server grant. Unconfirmed local grants expire after 24 hours.

The SSV endpoint validates the exact encoded query with ECDSA/SHA256 and Google's public key, refreshes cached keys hourly or on an unknown key ID, restricts the rewarded unit, and atomically consumes the nonce and unique transaction ID under the player lock. Replays of the same nonce/transaction succeed without another grant. The ledger records delta=0, reason=rewarded_refill, revenue=false; this is excluded from purchase revenue.

Reference: https://developers.google.com/admob/unity/ssv

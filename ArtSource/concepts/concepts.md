# Component art directions

## 1. Machined brass and enamel
Cream enamel keeps the ring body neutral for gameplay tinting.
Satin brass teeth, end caps, and clamp hardware give the mechanism a precise clockwork feel.
Shallow bevels and dark recesses define each part without relying on shine.

## 2. Soft matte clay
Rounded teeth and sculpted ends make the open ring feel like a tactile toy.
Warm chalk ring bodies pair with a muted clay holder and simple fastening details.
Broad shapes and soft contact shadows keep the gap and clamp readable at phone size.

## 3. Frosted glass and metal core
Milky, frosted outer surfaces sit over a solid pale metal structure.
Thick edges and satin highlights give the ring depth while preserving a light tintable base.
The holder uses the same sturdy geometry, with restrained metallic joints.

## 4. Bold-outline vector
Heavy ink outlines and flat grey shadow planes separate the ring from the dark field.
Pale cream rings remain tintable; warm copper marks the holder and mount.
Chunky teeth, clear cut ends, and a graphic clamp make the mechanism easy to scan.

# Orcub — pause/settings + lives prototypes

Approved style (see ref_approved_style.png and ref_current_game_ui.png): soft matte clay on dark plum felt;
cream clay slab top bar "Level 12 | 5 moves"; puffy clay pill buttons (mint = go, coral = special);
gear-wreath frame (coral + amber clay arcs joined by tan riveted clamps) for cards; Fredoka-style rounded
font; chunky clay stars; small brass clay cog = currency. Keep it CLEAN and UNCLUTTERED.

New components to prototype:
A. Top bar v2: add a round coral clay PAUSE button on the left end of the slab (restart button moves into
   the pause menu), keep "Level 12 | 5 moves".
B. Lives indicator: clay heart with a number (e.g. heart "5") — where it lives: either at the top-left above
   the slab, or inside the slab. Show options.
C. Pause / settings menu (opens from pause): inside the gear wreath or a cream clay card. Contains:
   "Paused" title, toggles for Sound and Haptics (clay switches), a "Restart" pill that shows it costs a life
   (heart -1), a "Resume" big mint pill. Clean.
D. Restart confirmation: "Restart level?" + "Uses 1 life" with a clay heart, "Restart" coral pill, "Cancel" text link.
E. Out of lives card: "Out of lives" + big clay heart with a timer "Next life in 24:31", "Refill with ad"
   coral pill (film icon), "Refill for 12 cogs" mint pill (brass cog), close/back text link.
Spell everything exactly as written.

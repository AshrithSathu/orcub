# Orcub UI — approved mix (for a combined mock-up)

The user approved these components from the four prototype sheets in this folder:
- Top bar (pause button, "Level 12", "5 moves", restart button): from ui_1.png — one cream clay slab bar,
  coral clay pause button on the left, green clay restart button on the right, divider between level and moves.
- Level complete card and Out of moves card: from ui_4.png — the "gear wreath": two open clay gear-rings
  (coral and amber) joined by tan riveted clamps, framing the content directly on the dimmed plum felt.
- Buttons inside the cards: from ui_1.png — thick puffy clay pills (coral / mint), with a film icon on the
  "Watch ad for +5 moves" button and a brass cog on the "+5 moves for 9 cogs" button; secondary actions
  ("Replay", "Give up / Retry") as smaller cream clay pills.
- Stars: from ui_1.png — chunky glossy-free clay stars, earned = butter yellow, unearned = tan clay.
- Coin / currency icon: from ui_4.png — small brass clay cog.
Text: Fredoka-style rounded font, plum or cream as in the references. Spell every word correctly.

- Level complete: thinner gear wreath with fewer teeth and two clamps; "Level 12", three clay stars (two earned),
  one big "Next" puffy pill, and a small cream text link "Replay" underneath. No coins line.
- Out of moves: same thin wreath; "Out of moves", ONE big coral pill "+5 moves" with a small film icon,
  and a small cream text link "Retry" underneath. No cogs button.

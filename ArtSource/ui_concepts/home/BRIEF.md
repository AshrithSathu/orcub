# Orcub — after-splash flow: loader, home, level map

Approved look (see ref_*.png): soft matte CLAY on dark PLUM FELT; pastel clay gear-rings; tan clay hardware with
rivets; cream clay slab bars; puffy clay pills (mint = go, coral = special); clay heart for lives; brass clay cog
coins; Fredoka-style rounded font; logo "orcub" with a coral gear "o" and tan clamp.
Flow: splash (logo) -> LOADER -> HOME -> LEVEL MAP / PLAY -> level -> result -> back home.
Everything must be ORIGINAL (do not imitate any existing game's map or home screen) and uncluttered.

Screens to prototype:
1. LOADER (2 options): plum felt, small "orcub" logo, a loading indicator made of our clay pieces (e.g. a gear-ring
   whose gap fills as it loads, or rings snapping onto a track), a one-line tip ("Tip: a locked ring is still holding
   another one").
2. HOME (3 distinct directions): top bar with lives heart + timer, brass cogs balance with a "+" (shop), settings.
   Big primary PLAY pill showing the next level ("Level 13").
   a) "Workshop": a cosy clay workbench scene behind the play button that the player restores/decorates with stars
      (meta progression), small task card "Fix the lamp - 3 stars".
   b) "Gear tower / map-first": home IS the level map - a winding path of clay gear stepping-stones going up the
      screen, current level glowing, finished levels show earned stars.
   c) your best idea.
   Optional bottom tab bar (Shop / Home / Events) if it fits the direction.
3. LEVEL MAP detail: 6-8 level nodes as clay gears on a path across the felt, completed ones with 1-3 small clay
   stars, the current one larger and glowing, locked ones dim with a tiny lock, a chapter banner ("Chapter 1 -
   The Workshop").
Spell every word exactly.

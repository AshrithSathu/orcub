# Orcub — logo + app icon brief

Game: "Orcub", a 2D mobile puzzle. Soft matte CLAY gear-rings (C-shaped open rings with chunky rounded
teeth and a gap) are joined by tan clay holders/clamps; you rotate rings until the gap reaches a clamp so it
pops off. Look at ArtSource/logo/game_reference.png for the exact art style: pastel clay rings
(coral, butter yellow, mint, sky blue, lavender, pink, teal, orange, periwinkle), tan clay hardware
(#D9AE86) with rivets, on a warm dark plum felt background.

Wordmark: the word "orcub" (lowercase preferred, may try Title case "Orcub" in one version).
Key idea: the letter "o" (and optionally the "c") is one of our clay gear-rings with its gap, and a tan
clamp holding it. Letters are chunky, rounded, soft matte clay, friendly and readable at small sizes.
Must be ORIGINAL — not the look of other puzzle apps; no neon, no glossy plastic.
Output must work on the plum felt background.
